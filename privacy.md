# Kids timer — Privacy Policy

Effective date: 13 October 2026

## Information handled by the app

Kids timer is an offline visual timer for children and their caregivers on
iOS and Android. The app does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal
information. It has no accounts, advertising, tracking, analytics, or
developer-operated crash reporting. It does not connect to a server or send
usage information to the developer or third parties.

The app does not request access to location, the camera, microphone, contacts,
Bluetooth, or notifications. Its timer visuals and completion sounds are
included with the app. Keeping the screen awake while a timer runs does not
collect information.

## Local settings and timer state

The only product setting saved between launches is whether the completion sound
is enabled. This is a boolean stored locally under the key
`completion_sound_enabled`; it is not an identifier and is not sent by the app.
Timer sessions, remaining time, and skin selection are held in memory, not saved
as a history or restored after the operating system ends the process.

You can change the sound setting in Parent settings. Removing the app's local
data removes this setting. The app does not implement cloud backup or
synchronization; device-level backup and restore may retain the preference
according to your operating system settings.

## Children

The app is designed for children and does not ask children to provide any
personal information. There are no accounts, messaging features, purchases,
advertisements, or links to external services in the app.
